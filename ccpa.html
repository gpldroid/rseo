CCPA / California Privacy Rights

Last updated: September 18, 2026

California privacy rights

California residents may have rights under applicable California privacy laws, including rights to know, access, correct, delete, and in certain circumstances opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information.

Submitting a request

To submit a privacy request, email info@fastseo.site. Please provide enough information for us to understand and process your request. We may take reasonable steps to verify requests where required by law.

No discrimination

We will not unlawfully discriminate against you for exercising privacy rights protected by applicable law.
