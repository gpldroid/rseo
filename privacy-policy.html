Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

Fastseo Website Reviewer respects your privacy and explains below what information may be processed when you use our website, audits, articles, and support channels. This notice should be read together with our GDPR information and California privacy information.

1. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the site, information may include:

  • URLs and audit settings that you voluntarily submit to the website reviewer.
  • Information you provide when contacting us, such as your email address and message.
  • Technical information made available by your browser or hosting infrastructure, such as IP address, device type, browser information, timestamps, and security logs, where applicable.
  • Preference information stored locally in your browser, such as a light/dark theme choice.

2. How submitted URLs are processed

When you request an audit, the submitted URL may be sent to Google PageSpeed Insights and related Google services needed to obtain Lighthouse or Chrome User Experience data. Do not submit confidential URLs, private credentials, personal data, or information you are not authorized to share.

3. Cookies and tracking technologies

Fastseo may use essential browser storage for preferences and security. Third-party resources such as Google services, analytics providers, advertising providers, or embedded content may use cookies, pixels, local storage, IP addresses, or similar technologies according to their own policies. If advertising or analytics that require consent are enabled, the site should obtain and record consent where legally required.

4. Analytics and third-party services

The website may use third-party infrastructure including Google PageSpeed Insights, Lighthouse/CrUX services, Google Fonts, Tailwind CSS CDN, hosting/CDN services, email providers, analytics, and advertising platforms. Each provider may process information under its own terms and privacy documentation. Where Google advertising products are used, their required privacy disclosures and consent mechanisms will apply.

5. Advertising and Google AdSense

If Google AdSense is enabled, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, or similar identifiers to serve and measure advertising. Users will be provided with the disclosures and consent choices required for the regions and advertising configuration involved. For users in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, personalized advertising requires the applicable Google-certified consent mechanism.

6. Purposes and legal bases

We may process information to provide requested audits, operate and secure the site, respond to support requests, prevent abuse, maintain records, improve content and tools, comply with legal obligations, and—where applicable—deliver analytics or advertising based on consent or another lawful basis.

7. Data retention and security

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, legal obligations, dispute resolution, and security. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Your privacy rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or obtain a copy of personal information, withdraw consent, and opt out of certain sale or sharing activities. California residents may also have rights under the CCPA/CPRA, including access, deletion, correction, opt-out, limitation, and non-discrimination rights.

To make a privacy request, email info@fastseo.site. We may request reasonable information to verify the request and will not use verification information for unrelated purposes.

9. Children's privacy

The website is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly request personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

10. Policy changes

We may update this policy when our services, vendors, legal requirements, or privacy practices change. The updated date at the top of this page will identify the latest revision.

This page is a practical privacy notice, not legal advice. Your actual compliance obligations depend on the services you operate, data you process, advertising configuration, and jurisdictions in which you operate.
