E-E-A-T & Content Transparency

Last updated: September 18, 2026
E-E-A-T stands for Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness and Trustworthiness. Google describes E-E-A-T as a set of qualities used to help identify helpful, reliable content; Google also states that E-E-A-T itself is not a specific ranking factor.

1. Who creates Fastseo content?

Fastseo Website Reviewer is operated by IMAD LIMRANI. The website publishes practical material about website performance, SEO auditing, Core Web Vitals and mobile user experience.

2. Experience

Our guides are designed around practical website-audit workflows and the metrics exposed by tools such as Lighthouse and PageSpeed Insights. Where a statement depends on a third-party tool or standard, we aim to identify the source.

3. Expertise

Technical explanations focus on established web-platform concepts, documented SEO guidance and measurable audit signals. Content should not be treated as professional legal, medical, financial or security advice.

4. Authoritativeness

We distinguish our own explanations from external documentation and link to primary sources where practical, including Google Search Central, web.dev, the European Commission, the California Privacy Protection Agency and the U.S. Copyright Office.

5. Trustworthiness

We aim to describe limitations, avoid promising rankings or guaranteed outcomes, keep important policy information accessible and provide a contact route for corrections or concerns.

6. Corrections and updates

If you identify an inaccurate or outdated statement, email info@fastseo.site with the page URL and the relevant point. We may review and update content when evidence or official guidance changes.

7. Editorial principle

Content is intended to be useful to people first. Meeting an SEO checklist does not guarantee visibility in search results, and automated tools are not substitutes for human review.

8. Official guidance

Read Google's current guidance on helpful, reliable, people-first content and E-E-A-T.
